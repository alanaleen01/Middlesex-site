Notable Towns in Middlesex County

Towns/Municipality Population (e.g. 2020) Fun Fact
Cambridge – 122,588 people – Many historical sites - George Washington took command of Cambridge in July 3, 1775.
Lowell – 119,971 people – First city in America to use phone numbers! Also, many good thrift/second hand shops and barns.
Newton – 91,483 people – It was America’s First Commuter Suburb!
Somerville – 81,045 people – It is the most densely populated municipality in New England.
Framingham – 72,952 people – Home of the groundbreaking Framingham Heart Study! Used by health professionals and medical students.
